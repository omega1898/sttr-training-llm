## Objective

Develop an explainable, adaptive capability for Navy workforce readiness, technical training, knowledge transfer and personnel development that combines personalized instruction with authoritative Navy workforce and training resources.

## Description

Workforce turnover and static materials slow readiness. Commercial models hallucinate and give no traceable sources on organization-specific content. The topic asks for a configurable, explainable language-model framework grounded in MPTE documents, with source-traceable answers that adapt to the user's role and update as materials change.

## Phase I

Feasibility study on sample Navy corpora (named types include training curricula, qualification standards, competency frameworks and technical manuals), covering fine-tuning and retrieval-augmented architecture and a hallucination-reduction approach. Deliverables: a technical feasibility report and a Phase II plan. The Q&A adds that a working proof of concept on representative or proxy data is expected.

## Phase II

A prototype training and knowledge-transfer platform with full corpus integration; adaptive instruction aligned to DoD Cyber Workforce Framework (DCWF) and Navy knowledge, skills, abilities and tasks; explainability and auditability with source citations; a deployment package for zero-trust governance; user evaluation of time to proficiency, retention and trust; interoperability with training management systems using xAPI and IEEE 1484.20.3. Deliverables: validated prototype, documentation and a transition plan.

## Phase III and dual use

Transition to Naval Education and Training Command and its training commands as an instructional tool, with expansion to other components and the Intelligence Community anticipated. Commercial uses in healthcare, finance, legal and critical infrastructure.
