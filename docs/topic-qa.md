# Posted topic Q&A: DON27TZ02-NV012 (summary)

Summarized from the public topic page ([navysbir.com](https://www.navysbir.com/n27_2/DON27TZ02-NV012.htm), read Nov 24, 2026). The official Q&A is in DSIP and closes to new questions Dec 16, 2026, 12:00 PM ET.

## Nov 11, 2026

- **Data for Phase I:** use only Distribution A material, such as curriculum documents, occupational standards, public training manuals, unrestricted qualification standards, personnel manuals and public instructions, plus synthetic learner data. No CUI in Phase I.
- **"Hyper-parameterized":** read it as domain-adapted and configurable.
- **Evaluation:** measure hallucination, explainability and instructional value against a baseline model.

## Nov 16, 2026

- **Working proof of concept in Phase I?** Yes: a prototype on representative or proxy data, plus the report.
- **Proxy data:** publicly releasable proxy data is acceptable.
- **Metrics:** no fixed thresholds; offerors propose their own.
- **Models and hosting:** open-weight models are favored; hosting tiers include IL5, on-premises and disconnected.
- **Pilot community:** medical and health-service training pipelines are in scope.
- **Explainability:** source citations are the minimum.
- **Zero trust and federated learning:** mainly Phase II and III.
- **Transition host:** no single system is specified.
